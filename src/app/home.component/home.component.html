Welcome to the Chatbot Talk Show

Click on the image of the Chatbot Talk Show for the video demo. The Chatbot Talk Show is a proof ofconcept on how to test a chatbot by using another chatbot. The unique part about this design is that itintegrates with speech technologies for a more natural conversation. The video is titled: Testing Chatbotsusing Chatbots with Google Dialogflow CX.
The Chatbot Talk Show has 3 modes of operation to test& exercise chatbots:  ○ Chatbot Host to Human Guest   ○ Human Host to Chatbot Guest   ○ Chatbot Hostto Chatbot Guest

Following are the Chatbot Talk Show features:
 ○ Integration with the Application   ○ Integrated Voice Response (IVR) without Telephony   ○ Real-timeOperation   ○ Full Duplex   ○ Voice Selection  ○ Different Testing Modes   ○ DialogFlow MessengerIntegration

Following are the Chatbot Talk Show future enhancements:
  ○ Group Discussions  ○ Language Translations   ○ Time Travel   ○ Different Interactive Games   ○ InteractiveLessons and Training
