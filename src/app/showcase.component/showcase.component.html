ChatMate Integration
The motivation for designing ChatMate was to offer a low-cost solution for integrating a chatbot directly into the application frame.

And then top it off with voice-activated commands and you will approach the future faster with more clarity.

The integration is simpler than most because it uses DialogFlow CX and the LLMs that are part of the Google’s Model Garden.

It automatically solves customer issues with safe, accurate, conversational answers based solely on your support content. Customer care is the heart of any organization.

ChatMate offers multiple agents and reusable dialog flow throughout your enterprise.
Smart Build for Translations
Smart Build is designed to streamline numerous software development tasks.

A prime example is its ability to handle multiple language translations during the build process, allowing developers to immediately see the results in action. This eliminates the need for real-time online translation, offering significant cost savings by shifting translation tasks offline during the build phase.

ProMatrix introduces Smart Build specifically for enhancing translation processes in software development.
